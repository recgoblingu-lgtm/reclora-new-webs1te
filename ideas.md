# RecLora preview design brief

## Direction
A grounded community-game revival site: unmistakably RecLora, energetic like a real player-run project, and polished without looking synthetic or over-produced. The original repository is the ground-truth source for the copy, imagery, icons, room captures, and community artwork.

## Principles
- Keep the original RecLora identity and all source assets in use; improve hierarchy, spacing, navigation, and readability rather than replacing the brand.
- Use a deep charcoal canvas with blue-green panels, pale text, and a Rec Room-inspired coral/orange accent.
- Make the site feel hand-curated: compact labels, practical copy, clear status badges, room imagery, and editorial cards.
- No AI references, generated-content notes, placeholder copy, or artificial-looking visual effects.

## Layout
A sticky near-black navigation bar, logo-led hamburger drawer on mobile, wide image-led hero, narrow readable content column, and modular cards with small uppercase metadata. Secondary pages use the same shell and visual language.

## Typography
Use system sans with a sturdy display weight for headings and a slightly tighter body rhythm. Headings are bold and compact; labels are uppercase and letter-spaced.

## Signature elements
Coral action buttons, lime status dots, clipped corner accents, room-photo crops, and small asset-backed feature tiles using the repository's Home, Rooms, People, Events, Credit, and Settings icons.

## Interaction
Fast and obvious. Hover states lift cards slightly, links underline or brighten, and the existing mobile menu remains available. Avoid excessive animation.
